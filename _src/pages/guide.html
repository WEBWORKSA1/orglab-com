Part 1: The 10-step checklist

  • ☐ 1. Confirm 36-month land history (no prohibited substances)
  • ☐ 2. Choose target standards & markets (NOP / COR / EU)
  • ☐ 3. Shortlist 2–3 accredited certifiers & request quotes
  • ☐ 4. Draft the Organic System Plan (OSP)
  • ☐ 5. Compile the input list (fertilizers, pest controls, sanitizers) with approval status
  • ☐ 6. Prepare field and facility maps with buffer zones
  • ☐ 7. Set up records: seed sourcing, activity logs, harvest/lot, sales
  • ☐ 8. Submit the application & fees; respond to review questions
  • ☐ 9. Pass the on-site inspection (mock audit first)
  • ☐ 10. Get labels approved → certificate → claim cost-share

Part 2: Document checklist

  • Land use history & affidavits (36 months)
  • Maps: fields, buffers, water sources, storage
  • Seed & planting stock sources (organic or documented non-availability)
  • Input labels / OMRI or certifier approval evidence
  • Pest, weed & disease management plan
  • Soil fertility & crop rotation plan
  • Livestock health, feed and pasture records (if applicable)
  • Sanitation, pest control & commingling-prevention plan (handlers)
  • Supplier organic certificates (handlers and brands)
  • Label artwork drafts
