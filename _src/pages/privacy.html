Privacy Policy

Last updated {{TODAY}}

1. Who we are

OrgLab.com (“OrgLab”, “we”) operates this website. Contact: our contact page.

2. What we collect

  • Information you give us: name, email, phone, company, operation details and messages submitted through forms (newsletter, certification quotes, contests, job applications, listings, contact).
  • Usage data: pages viewed, device and browser type, approximate location and referral source, collected through analytics cookies if you consent.
  • Local storage: tool preferences, theme choice and form drafts, stored in your browser only.

3. How we use it

To respond to requests, deliver newsletters and downloads, run contests, match certification and testing requests with partners (only with your explicit consent on that form), improve the site and show advertising.

4. Sharing with partners

When you submit a certification or testing quote request and tick the consent box, we share your request details with up to 3 matched certifiers, consultants or laboratories so they can contact you. We never sell newsletter lists.

5. Cookies & advertising

We use Google AdSense to show ads. Google and its partners use cookies to serve ads based on your visits to this and other sites. You can opt out of personalized advertising at Google Ads Settings or aboutads.info. We use Google Consent Mode: ad and analytics cookies stay off until you consent through our banner. For visitors in the EEA, UK and Switzerland, a Google-certified consent management platform is used where required.

6. Third-party services

Google (AdSense, Analytics, Fonts), YouTube (privacy-enhanced embeds that load only when you click play), our form processor, and payment processors (Stripe, PayPal, Buy Me a Coffee, GitHub Sponsors) handle data under their own policies. We never see or store your full card details.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live (GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, PIPEDA, Quebec Law 25 and others), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or port your data, to object to or restrict processing, and to withdraw consent. California residents may opt out of the “sale” or “sharing” of personal information. Contact us to exercise any of these rights; we respond within 30 days.

8. Retention & security

We keep data only as long as needed for the purpose it was collected for, and we protect it with reasonable technical and organizational safeguards.

9. Children

This site is not directed to children under 13. Youth contest entries need a parent or guardian's consent.

10. Changes

We will post any changes here and update the date above.
