FAQ

Quick answers

Is OrgLab a certifier?
No. OrgLab is an independent information and testing-coordination platform. We help you understand labels and connect operations with USDA-accredited certifiers and accredited labs.
Are your lab results paid for by brands?
Never. Brands can buy ads or sponsor newsletters, but results, scores and rankings are not for sale. See our methodology.
Is organic food actually healthier?
Organic consistently means lower synthetic pesticide residues; nutrition differences are smaller and mixed. Read the evidence review.
How much does organic certification cost?
Small operations typically pay roughly $1,000–$3,000 in year one before cost-share. Use the estimator for your numbers.
