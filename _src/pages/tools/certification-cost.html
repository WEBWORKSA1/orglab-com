What drives your certification cost

1. Annual fee by sales: most certifiers scale the annual fee with gross organic sales, so it's the biggest variable for growing brands. 2. Inspection: billed hourly or flat, plus travel, and more sites mean more time. 3. Complexity: multiple scopes, co-packers, imports and add-on standards all add review time.

Is the estimate a quote?
No. It's an indicative range. Fees differ a lot between certifiers, which is why comparing 2–3 quotes usually saves money.
How can I lower my costs?
Apply for cost-share, keep records clean to avoid re-inspections, combine standards in a single inspection, and pick a certifier with inspectors near you to cut travel charges.
