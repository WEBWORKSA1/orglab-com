🥬 Buy in season

In-season organic produce often costs close to conventional, especially at farmers' markets.

🧊 Go frozen

Frozen organic berries and spinach cost much less than fresh and keep their nutrients.

🧺 Join a CSA

A farm share can cut the cost per pound. Find one near you.
