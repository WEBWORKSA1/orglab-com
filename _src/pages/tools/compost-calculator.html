Compost C:N Ratio Mixer

Hot, fast, odour-free compost comes down to one number: a carbon-to-nitrogen ratio of about 25–35:1. Mix your materials and see where you land.

🟢 Greens (nitrogen)

Food scraps, grass clippings, coffee grounds, fresh manure.

🟤 Browns (carbon)

Dry leaves, straw, cardboard, wood chips, sawdust.

💧 Moisture

Aim for “wrung-out sponge”, about 50–60%. Too wet goes anaerobic; too dry stalls.
