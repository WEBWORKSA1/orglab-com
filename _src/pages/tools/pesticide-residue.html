    FAQ

    Does washing remove pesticides?
    Rinsing under running water removes some surface residues. Soaking in a baking-soda solution removes more for some pesticides, but systemic pesticides inside the plant tissue stay. Peeling helps but also removes nutrients.
    Is organic produce pesticide-free?
    No. Organic farming allows a limited list of mostly natural pesticides, and drift can occur. Organic produce does consistently show far fewer and lower synthetic pesticide residues.
    Should I avoid conventional high-risk produce entirely?
    No. The health benefits of fruits and vegetables outweigh residue risks. Use this list to prioritize organic purchases, not to eat less produce.
