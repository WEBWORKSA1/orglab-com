About OrgLab

We test the claims so you don't have to guess

OrgLab (“The Organic Lab”) is an independent platform that turns lab data, regulation and research into clear, practical answers for people who buy, grow and make organic.

Our mission

Organic is a roughly $70-billion market in the US alone, and it runs on trust. Labels multiply, claims blur and certification looks intimidating from the outside. We make it transparent. Consumers learn where organic matters and where it doesn't. Growers and brands get a clear, affordable path to certification.

What we do

  • Test: independent lab testing of foods and products bought at retail.
  • Build tools: free calculators, checkers and planners.
  • Explain: cited guides that are reviewed and dated.
  • Connect: matching farms and brands with accredited certifiers and labs.

Our promises

  • Test results are never for sale.
  • Sponsored content is always labelled.
  • Every guide cites its sources.
  • We correct errors openly and date the corrections.
  • Affiliate links never change our rankings.

Methodology

How we test

  1. Selection

    We pick categories by reader votes, search demand and public-health relevance, and choose products by market share and reader nominations.

  2. Anonymous retail purchase

    We buy products from ordinary retailers and online stores, as consumers would. Brands are never told in advance.

  3. Blind accredited lab analysis

    Samples are recoded and sent to ISO/IEC 17025-accredited labs: multi-residue pesticide screens (LC-MS/MS, GC-MS/MS), glyphosate/AMPA, heavy metals by ICP-MS, and label-claim verification.

  4. Scoring

    Scores (0–100) weight heavy metals (35%), pesticide residues (30%), label accuracy (20%) and other contaminants (15%) against published reference levels. Grades run from A to D.

  5. Right of reply & publication

    Brands get 10 business days to comment before we publish. We publish certificates of analysis for members.

Editorial policy

Guides are written by the OrgLab Research Desk from peer-reviewed literature and regulatory sources, and reviewed before publication. Every article shows its last-reviewed date. Found an error? Report it. We aim to review every correction request within 5 business days.

How we make money

Display ads, affiliate links (disclosed), sponsorships, reader memberships and donations, and referral fees from certification and testing partners. None of these can buy results, scores or rankings. Full details are in our disclosure.
